CUSTOMER CHURN & RETENTION ANALYTICS
Excel Analysis Findings

1. Overall Customer Churn
Total Customers: 7,043
Customers Who Left: 1,869
Customers Who Stayed: 5,174
Churn Rate: 26.54%
Retention Rate: 73.46%

2. Churn by Contract Type
Month-to-month contracts have the highest churn rate (42.71%).
One-year contracts have a churn rate of 11.27%.
Two-year contracts have the lowest churn rate (2.83%).

3. Churn by Internet Service
Fiber optic customers have the highest churn rate (41.89%).
DSL customers have a churn rate of 18.96%.
Customers without internet service have a churn rate of 7.40%.

4. Churn by Tenure
Customers in their first 12 months show the highest churn rate.
Churn rates generally decrease as customer tenure increases.

Business Recommendations
- Encourage customers to choose longer-term contracts.
- Investigate why fiber optic customers have higher churn.
- Improve onboarding and support for new customers.
- Introduce targeted retention offers for customers at risk of leaving.
